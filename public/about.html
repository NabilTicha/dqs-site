Delft Quant Society

Delft Quant Society is an independent student society at TU Delft, founded in Fall 2025. Our purpose is to help TU Delft students prepare for recruitment at quantitative trading and research firms.

This site hosts our practice material: timed tests modelled on the assessments these firms use, and a leaderboard for TU Delft students.
